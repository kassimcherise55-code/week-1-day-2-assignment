1. Platform Overview

FastSend is a next-generation financial platform built specifically for Kenyan businesses and consumers. We bridge the gap between traditional banking and mobile wallets through instant, low-cost APIs.

Whether operating a retail store in Mombasa or a digital software enterprise in Nairobi, FastSend guarantees zero settlement delays and automated reconciliations.

2. Payment Solutions

Support all customer payment preferences with a single integration. FastSend accepts MPesa, Airtel Money, Visa, Mastercard, and direct bank transfers from all major Kenyan banks.

Transactions are processed instantly with real-time webhooks notifying your application immediately upon receipt.

3. Security & Compliance

Security is built into every layer of our network infrastructure. FastSend complies with national regulations set by the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) and Data Protection Act standards.

We perform daily automated security scans, end-to-end tokenization, and multi-factor authentication across all developer accounts.

4. API Integration

Our RESTful APIs allow developers to go live in less than 30 minutes. SDKs are readily available for Node.js, Python, PHP, and Android platforms.

Test your integration seamlessly using our sandbox environment with test credentials and simulated MPesa payments.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions regarding FastSend services below.

How fast are transaction payouts?

All transfers to MPesa wallets and local Kenyan bank accounts are processed in real-time (under 5 seconds).

Are there any monthly registration fees?

No! FastSend operates on a transparent pay-as-you-go model. You only pay a small percentage per successful transaction.

Is FastSend regulated in Kenya?

Yes, FastSend complies strictly with the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) guidelines and national data privacy laws.

Can I integrate FastSend into my website?

Absolutely. We provide simple WooCommerce plugins, Shopify add-ons, and clean RESTful APIs for custom applications.

How can I contact FastSend Support?

Our customer support team is available 24/7 via live chat, email (support@fastsend.co.ke), or WhatsApp (+254 700 000 000).
